Everything you need to know before we collaborate

Clear answers regarding my design process, developer handoffs, sprint turnaround times, pricing frameworks, and post-launch support.

⚡ Process & Sprints
Turnaround depends on the scope of your product, but here are typical industry benchmarks I operate with:
  • Targeted 2-Week Design Sprint: Ideal for redesigning an onboarding funnel, a checkout flow, or a clickable investor MVP pitch deck.
  • Standard Mobile App (10–18 screens): Approximately 4 to 6 weeks from kickoff discovery to final developer token handoff.
  • Full B2B SaaS Platform or Multi-Platform Ecosystem: Typically 7 to 10 weeks, encompassing user journeys, component libraries, and interactive prototypes.
I mitigate the risk of surprises by breaking projects into milestone checkpoints:

First, we align on wireframes and information architecture. Next, I share 2 distinct moodboard directions for visual styling. Only after you approve the aesthetic foundation do we proceed to full high-fidelity screen design. All fixed packages include 2 structured rounds of revisions per milestone, plus lightweight real-time feedback via Figma comments.
My primary workspace is Figma (using modern auto-layout, nested component properties, and token variables). For micro-interactions and complex logic prototypes, I leverage ProtoPie and Framer. For user research, roadmapping, and async updates, I use Miro, Notion, Maze, and Loom.
💻 Dev Handoff & Engineering
I take pride in delivering developer-ready files that engineers genuinely appreciate. Every deliverable includes:
  • Semantic Design Tokens: Spacing (8pt grid), color roles (surface, primary, on-brand), typography scales, and radius variables.
  • Edge & Error States: Empty views, loading skeletons, modal timeouts, and character validation errors.
  • Exportable Assets: Clean, optimized SVGs with unified viewboxes and WebP assets.
  • Live Walkthrough: A 45-minute handoff call with your tech leads to review interaction behavior and answer questions.
My primary role is product architecture and UI/UX design. However, having a solid background in modern semantic HTML, CSS Grid/Flexbox, and responsive ergonomics means I design with 100% technical feasibility in mind. Your developers will never receive unrealistic layouts or unbuildable motion curves.
Yes! Every project includes 14 days of complimentary Design QA support after handoff. I will audit your staging/test environments, verify margins, font rendering, and micro-interactions, and log any visual discrepancies directly in your team’s Jira, GitHub, or Linear queue.
💳 Pricing & Contracts
For fixed-scope projects, payments are typically split into milestones: 50% upfront deposit upon contract execution to reserve queue capacity, and 50% upon final prototype & token handoff (or 40/30/30 for enterprise scopes).

For monthly retainer contracts, billing is handled at the beginning of each 30-day billing cycle. Invoices can be paid in USD, EUR, GBP, or INR via Stripe, Wise, or Direct Wire.
I strongly prefer value-driven, fixed-price contracts or dedicated sprints. Fixed pricing provides total budget certainty for your team and aligns our incentives around shipping exceptional quality rather than running up hours.
